Neuropaediatric referral

A 15-month-old girl was referred for motor delay. She cannot sit without support and does not yet babble. Examination showed marked hypotonia. Overall the picture is consistent with global developmental delay. Head circumference is at the 2nd percentile; microcephaly is possible but should be confirmed by a repeated measurement. Feeding was difficult in the first months of life. There have been no seizures.
